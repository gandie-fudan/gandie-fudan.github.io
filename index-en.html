Assistant Professor A (Researcher) · PhD Supervisor

My research focuses on adaptive identification and control of stochastic systems, with interests in distributed learning, sparse identification and their mathematical foundations. I joined Fudan University in June 2026, after serving as an Associate Professor at Nankai University.

01 / RESEARCH AREAS

Research interests

Mathematical foundations of stochastic systems and learning

01

Distributed adaptive learning & control

How networked agents learn, estimate and control stochastic systems using local data.

02

Sparse identification

Identifying essential parameters and sparse structures in stochastic dynamical systems from noisy observations.

03

Federated & reinforcement learning

Learning from distributed data and studying feedback-driven decision methods.

04

Mathematics & artificial intelligence

Exploring the mathematical foundations of learning and estimation.

02 / EXPLORING NEXT

Prospective research interest

System identification for dexterous hands

I am interested in extending stochastic system identification and adaptive estimation to dexterous hands, exploring how joint motion and contact feedback can inform models that evolve with the system.

QUESTIONS TO EXPLORE

01

Joint & actuator dynamics

How can friction, coupling and actuator properties be identified in a model suitable for online estimation?

02

Contact & changing parameters

How can key parameters and uncertainty be estimated under changing contact conditions and noisy observations?

03

Identification & adaptive control

How can online model updates inform control, with analysis of estimation and stability in closed loop?
