Brief biography

My research focuses on adaptive identification and control of stochastic systems, with interests in distributed learning, sparse identification and their mathematical foundations. I joined Fudan University in June 2026, after serving as an Associate Professor at Nankai University.

I received my B.Sc. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Shandong University in 2017 and my Ph.D. from the Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences, in 2022. I was a Postdoctoral Fellow at Zhongguancun Laboratory from 2022 to 2024 and an Associate Professor at Nankai University from 2024 to 2026. Full background →

Research interests

  • Distributed adaptive learning & control — How networked agents learn, estimate and control stochastic systems using local data.
  • Sparse identification — Identifying essential parameters and sparse structures in stochastic dynamical systems from noisy observations.
  • Federated & reinforcement learning — Learning from distributed data and studying feedback-driven decision methods.
  • Mathematics & artificial intelligence — Exploring the mathematical foundations of learning and estimation.

Prospective interest: System identification for dexterous hands. I am interested in extending stochastic system identification and adaptive estimation to dexterous hands, exploring how joint motion and contact feedback can inform models that evolve with the system.
