Brief biography

My research focuses on adaptive identification and control of stochastic systems, with interests in distributed learning, sparse identification and their mathematical foundations. I joined Fudan University in June 2026, after serving as an Associate Professor at Nankai University.

I received my B.Sc. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Shandong University in 2017 and my Ph.D. from the Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences, in 2022. I was a Postdoctoral Fellow at Zhongguancun Laboratory from 2022 to 2024 and an Associate Professor at Nankai University from 2024 to 2026. Full background →

Research interests

  • Sparse identificationIdentifying essential parameters and sparse structures in stochastic dynamical systems from noisy observations.
  • Federated & reinforcement learningLearning from distributed data and studying feedback-driven decision methods.
  • Mathematics & artificial intelligenceExploring the mathematical foundations of learning and estimation.

Future research: System identification for dexterous hands

I plan to extend my research on stochastic system identification, distributed learning and adaptive estimation to dexterous hands. The central question is how motion and contact data can inform models useful for control. Starting with joint and actuator identification, I aim to explore multi-finger coordination, changing contact conditions and closed-loop learning, with an emphasis on interpretable models, online adaptation and theoretical analysis of estimation errors.

01

Joint, actuator and inter-finger identification

I aim to use observations such as joint positions, velocities and actuator inputs to study parameterized descriptions of friction, transmission effects and coupling between joints. Combining physical models with sparse identification may help isolate dynamics relevant to prediction and control. A related focus would be data excitation and parameter identifiability.

02

Changing contact and online adaptive estimation

I plan to investigate online model updates as contact states, loads and object properties change during grasping and manipulation, using motion, force or tactile feedback where available. Questions include estimation with noisy, correlated and nonstationary observations, and whether information shared across joints and fingers can improve parameter tracking and uncertainty characterization.

03

Identification and control in closed loop

I aim to study how online model estimates can support adaptive control, and how estimation error and closed-loop stability can be analyzed when feedback influences the observed data. A further question is how to balance task execution with information gathering through informative actions subject to constraints.

Proposed research approach

The proposed path progresses from simplified models and identifiability analysis to online estimation in simulation and then task-oriented closed-loop validation. I would begin with a single joint and a small set of coupled joints before extending to multi-finger contact. Evaluation would consider parameter estimation error, prediction accuracy, tracking performance and constraint satisfaction. Experimental platforms and tasks remain to be determined as the research develops.
